## About

**Heaven's Cake** is an FSSAI-registered (`21322210000116`) homemade bakery based in Karimba, Mannarkkad, Palakkad, Kerala. Since 2020, we have crafted fresh, made-to-order celebration cakes, cupcakes, brownies, jar cakes, and gift hampers for over **2,500+ happy customers**.

## Highlights

- **Interactive 3D Hero Showcase:** Smooth coverflow cake gallery powered by Swiper.js.
- **Dynamic Menu & Instant Search:** Real-time search and filtering across cakes, desserts, and hampers via `data/products.json`.
- **Custom Cake Pricing Guide:** Clear breakdown for designer cakes (Doll, Car, Heart/Square shapes), fondant artistry, and ½ kg custom weights.
- **Verified Customer Reviews:** Star ratings and customer feedback backed by **Firebase Authentication** (Email & Google Sign-In) and **Cloud Firestore**.
- **Seamless Ordering:** Direct one-tap WhatsApp order links pre-filled with the selected cake.
- **PWA & Push Notifications:** Installable Progressive Web App manifest with OneSignal web notifications.
